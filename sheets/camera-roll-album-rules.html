Camera Roll Album Rules

Four albums and five rules, so your photos organize themselves and you can actually find the picture of that receipt.

Pacing 10 minutes of setup today; after that the rules run themselves on every reset day. Set it up once, then coast.

Make these four albums

  • Gold — the keep-forever shelf. A handful of pictures per month, max.
  • People — faces you love, travel buddies, the crew.
  • Paper — photos of receipts, forms, prescription labels, parking spots, appliance plates.
  • Watch This — screenshots with an action attached: a recipe, a door code, a discount code.

The five rules

  1. Gold stays small. If you flag more than about ten photos a month, "Gold" stops meaning anything. Scarcity is what makes it the shelf.
  2. Paper photos get thrown away once acted on. Filed the receipt? Reimbursed the form? Out it goes. The album is a to-do shelf, not a museum.
  3. Watch This empties every reset day. Each month, do every screenshot that is waiting, then clear them out. (If one has been sitting three months, admit you are not doing it and delete it guilt-free.)
  4. People gets one slow look per reset day. Skim last month's people photos and pick one worth printing or sending on.
  5. Everything else lives in the timeline. The main roll is storage; albums are for finding. You never have to sort the timeline, and that is the whole trick.

One-time setup checklist

  • Create the four albums (2 min).
  • Move a first handful of favorites into Gold and People (5 min). Resist reorganizing history; it is endless and the point is forward-motion.
