Duplicate Photo Triage

Turn a thousand near-identical photos into a few hundred you would actually look at twice.

Pacing Two 10-minute rounds with a stretch in between. Never scroll deeper than your thumb gets bored; this sheet works in dabs, not marathons.

Round 1: obvious trash (no hard feelings allowed)

  • Blurry, dark, or "my pocket was very artistic" shots.
  • Screenshots that duplicated a photo on purpose (menus, listings, weather).
  • Anything so old it belongs to a previous address book of a life.

Deletions this round should feel boring. That is the goal.

Round 2: bursts and poses

  • Bursts: keep the one frame where everything looks decent, delete the rest. A ten-photo burst usually keeps one survivor.
  • Same pose, several attempts: "never keep twins" — pick the winner while you are still on the pair, delete the other before scrolling on.

Round 3: the sentimental stall

Found one you cannot delete yet? Star it, add it to Gold, and move on. Triage is not the moment for life decisions; that is what the Gold album is for.

Why bother: near-duplicates are the quiet bulk of most camera rolls. Removing just the twins and burst leftovers typically frees up gigabytes, and future-you gets to page through actual memories instead of the same memory sixteen times.

Done looks like: a roll where every swipe shows something new, and a tally you can be smug about. Remember to empty Recently Deleted before you claim the space.
