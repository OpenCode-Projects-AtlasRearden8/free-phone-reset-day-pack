The App Purge Decision Card

A fast yes/no card for every app on your phone, so you never have to think about a single app for more than ten seconds.

Pacing 10 to 15 minutes of rapid-fire questions, no deliberating allowed. This is a great standing-at-the-counter sheet; go app by app and trust the first honest answer.

The four questions (ask in order, out loud if needed)

Q1. Have I opened this in the last 30 days?

Q2. Does it do one real thing a browser cannot?

Q3. Does it quietly cost me money every month?

Q4. If it vanished today, would I reinstall it within a week?

The ladder

DELETE Q1 is no and Q4 is no. It was a phase. Phases should not live on your home screen.

HOLD Q1 is no but Q4 is yes (or you are unsure). Move it off the home screen into a folder named "Someday". If you never open the folder by next reset day, it graduates to delete.

KEEP It does a real job (Q2), or it is your reminder home, or opening it feels like a good habit. No guilt attached.

Your roll call

Walk through your app drawer alphabetically. Eight rows at a time is plenty for one sitting.

App Keep Hold Delete

Comfort option: many phones can offload an app instead of deleting it: the icon goes away, the data stays. It is the Hold folder, but for an app.

Done looks like: a home screen where every icon earns its seat, and one "Someday" folder on a short leash.
