The Subscription Hunt

Find every recurring charge that slipped its leash, then decide on purpose what stays.

Pacing About 20 minutes and it needs your bank statement, so this is a proper desk-sheet with a warm drink. Do the hunt spots one at a time; each one is only a couple of minutes.

Hunt spots (check them all)

  • The app store's Subscriptions page — the official list of what is charging you through the store.
  • Your bank statement, last 90 days — look for small identical amounts that repeat. Those small ones add up fastest.
  • Email search for "receipt", "renewal", "subscription", "invoice" — catches anything not billed through the store.
  • Payment apps and wallets (PayPal, Venmo and friends) — recurring charges hide here too.
  • Family and shared plans — ask whoever manages the household plan what you are actually paying for.

What Cost Renews Keep Cancel Unsure

Gotchas worth knowing

  • Yearly plans masquerade as small ones. A $60 charge each year looks "cheap" next to monthly ones; divide by twelve before deciding.
  • Free trials you forgot to notice. For any trial still running, set a calendar reminder two days before the charge lands.
  • Cancelled is not the same as removed. Cancelling on the store page is not the end; confirm you got the cancellation email.
