The Storage Sweep

Find out what is actually eating your phone's space, and clear the pile you can clear today.

Pacing About 15 minutes, and it pauses well. Do the start step, note your number, and if you get tired, the checkboxes will happily wait for tomorrow's you.

Start here: take the before picture (3 min)

  • Open your phone's Settings → Storage (or the storage panel your phone has).
  • Write down your free space and stare at the biggest bar for a moment. That bar is today's target.

The big three (5 min)

These three spots hide the most junk per minute of effort.

  • Downloads folder. Sort by newest, delete everything older than a month that you cannot name on sight.
  • Chat app attachments. Open your messaging app's storage screen and clear old photo/video attachments. (They live there even if you saved the good ones.)
  • "Downloaded for later" pile. Podcasts, shows, maps, and offline music you finished or forgot. Keep one episode, delete the rest.
