Notification Trims, One Page

One rule, one table, and ten minutes. After this your phone speaks only when someone actually wants you.

Pacing A single 10-minute sit-down, phone in hand on the couch. No need to be thorough — the table decides for you, so just march down it top to bottom.

The one rule: sound is for people. Everything else can wait in silence.

Trim by category

Category Recommended setting Done
Social apps All off except direct messages from real people. No likes, no "trending".
Shopping Everything off. Sales will still find you when you open the app on purpose.
Games Everything off, including "your energy is full!". Play when you want, not when pinged.
News Off, or one digest per day at most. Breaking news is not usually breaking.
Banking & travel Keep security alerts and traveling statuses only. Turn off marketing nudges.
Messages from people Keep banners and sounds. This is the one channel that gets to be loud.
Calendar & alarms Alerts for real commitments only. Reminders you actually set: keep. Anything the app invents: trim.
