The Backup Happiness Check

Five checks that tell you whether dropping this phone in a soup would be a Bad Day or a Mild Inconvenience.

Pacing 15 minutes, with the phone on a charger while you work. Plug it in first; backups love a full battery and so will you.

The five checks

  • Last automatic backup: within the past 7 days? Write the date: If it is older, start the backup now and let it run while you do the rest of the pack.
  • Photos live in two places: cloud and something you own. Once a season, copy this month's photos to a computer or drive and tick this:
  • Contacts actually synced: sign in on any other device or the contacts website and confirm a few names are there. Write the check-time:
  • Second copies of the keys: security codes for your important accounts are not stored only on this one phone. Backup codes printed or saved elsewhere:
  • The taste test: open one backed-up file (a photo, a note) in its new home and confirm it is really you, really there.

The happiness meter

If you scored low: that is exactly what this sheet is for, and you are scoring high next month. Run the backup while it charges, do the taste test once it finishes, and colour that box in with pride.

Done looks like: a date less than a week old, photos in two places, and a number you have earned. This is the quietest way to be a hero to future-you.
