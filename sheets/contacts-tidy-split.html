The Contacts Tidy

Three short passes so your address book reads like a list of lives, not a junk drawer.

Pacing Split into three 5-minute passes. Do one now, one while you wait for something later, one after dinner. Snack-sized on purpose.

First: the safety net (1 min)

  • Export or back up your contacts before touching anything — most contact apps can save a copy to your account in one tap. THEN edit bravely.

Pass 1: the delete sweep

  • Old jobs, closed restaurants, the gym you left in 2021.
  • "Pizza Delivery 2019", "Plumber ????", and other entries that now mean nothing.
  • Numbers you transcribed and never needed again.

Pass 2: the merge

  • Find people with two cards (one from a sync, one from an import). Merge them — keep the card your messaging app already knows.
  • Pick which number stays primary: usually the one that actually works. Two numbers per person max.

Pass 3: the upgrade

Add the handful of people whose numbers you always search for. Future-you will type a name instead of fighting an autocomplete ghost.

Done looks like: no duplicate cards, no ghost entries, and the five real numbers that matter, saved with real names. Your address book now weighs less than your phone does.
